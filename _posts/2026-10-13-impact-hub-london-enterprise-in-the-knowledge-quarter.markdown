This week, we’re taking a closer look at **Impact Hub** and the entrepreneurs working within its community. Its Euston home connects with a wider story about the *Knowledge Quarter*, where universities, research institutes, cultural organisations and businesses sit within walking distance of one another.

<!--read-->

#### Inside the Knowledge Quarter

Impact Hub London’s [Euston workspace](https://london.impacthub.net/euston), at 1 Triton Square, is close to King’s Cross and sits within the [Knowledge Quarter](https://www.knowledgequarter.london/). The area brings together organisations such as the British Library, UCL, Wellcome and the Francis Crick Institute, with [Meta’s offices at King’s Cross](https://about.fb.com/news/2022/03/the-prince-of-wales-and-the-duchess-of-cornwall-are-officially-opening-metas-london-kings-cross-offices/) also nearby. For entrepreneurs, this concentration of research, technology and culture offers opportunities to exchange ideas, find collaborators and build contacts.

#### Businesses within the community

Impact Hub London’s community and business programmes span several industries. Examples include:

**Climate and sustainability**

- **[Ailuna](https://ailuna.com/):** Develops an app and platform that helps employees and customers build more sustainable habits. Its programmes use behavioural science and practical challenges to encourage changes such as saving energy and reducing waste. It also supports cyber security awareness and fraud prevention.
- **[ASSETS 2025 cohort](https://london.impacthub.net/introducing-the-assets-2025-cohort):** Through this programme with Wates, Impact Hub London supported enterprises working on low-carbon heating and sustainable construction. Participants included **CAFgas CIC**, a plumbing and heating social enterprise that reinvests revenue in community projects; **Limitless Energy**, which installs solar panels, heat pumps and batteries; and **CYD Innovation**, which advises on housing retrofit projects.

**Fintech**

- **[Singlify](https://www.singlify.app/):** Builds core banking platforms on Salesforce for credit unions and community lenders. Its software helps these organisations modernise their systems and serve people who may struggle to access mainstream financial services.

**Media**

- **[The Grove Media](https://thegrovemedia.co.uk/):** An independent, B Corp certified media planning and buying agency. It helps clients across different sectors decide where to advertise and how to use their advertising budgets.

#### Turning purpose into a viable business

For a social enterprise, the commercial questions can be familiar: who will pay for the service, how much will it cost to deliver and how will the organisation keep going? Alongside these sit questions about the social or environmental changes it wants to achieve.

Impact Hub London’s [business programmes](https://london.impacthub.net/programmes) support entrepreneurs with mentoring, opportunities to test ideas and preparation for funding. Its work covers climate action, health and inclusion.

Past programmes have supported circular economy businesses and helped Camden and Islington residents develop enterprises addressing local health needs. Its current Together for Wellbeing programme supports entrepreneurs developing preventative mental health solutions.

Access is also part of the hub’s work. When it opened in Euston, it introduced free memberships for Camden residents and businesses, creating a route into the community for people who might otherwise struggle to afford workspace.

#### Meet the founders and the Impact Hub team

During the visit, our group will meet **two founders and the Impact Hub management team**. Students can ask the founders about developing their ideas, finding customers and keeping their businesses financially viable while pursuing their social or environmental goals.

Meeting the management team also opens up questions about the hub itself: how it supports its members, connects entrepreneurs and creates opportunities for collaboration.

For students exploring enterprise, these conversations reach into the wider neighbourhood. How does an idea find its customers? Who helps it develop? And how can a district rich in knowledge create opportunities for the people living around it?
